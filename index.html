Methodology Note: Downside Risk vs. Total Volatility

The classical Sharpe Ratio evaluates excess return against total standard deviation (σ), treating outsized positive trading days and crash events identically. This terminal prioritizes the Sortino Ratio, isolating downside deviation (σd) by considering only returns falling below the sovereign risk-free rate. Positive volatility is never penalized.
